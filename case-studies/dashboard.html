Four systems wired into one buying-decisions screen, with an agent doing the overnight legwork.

SLATE PROBLEM BUILD PROOF NEXT

Problem

Stock lives in your head, wants live in Notion, spend lives in Ledger, and deals live on forums. Buying well means joining all four, which nobody does at 9pm.

What I built

Stock stateHome Assistant tracks consumables; NFC tags on the shelf mark things low with one phone tap.
Wants listNotion database of things worth waiting for a deal on, with target prices.
Spend contextRead-only queries against Ledger's Supabase, so a purchase is seen against the month's budget.
Deal agentA scheduled Claude agent parses deal feeds and price-history data overnight, matching against the wants list and flagging genuine drops.

The NFC layer: physical world in, one tap

An NFC tag on the pantry shelf, tapped with a phone, fires a Shortcut into a Home Assistant webhook and flips an input_boolean. Low-stock alerts and the buy list hang off that flag. A Home Assistant dashboard is parked beside the energy and climate cards.

What it shows

  • The value is the join: stock, wants, spend, and deals on one screen.
  • Core views are already live with the household. The rest is parked.
