Dailies Importer

SC MORNINGTK 148FPS 24IN 00:00:00:00 OUT 05:09:57:21
ROLE Design, build, daily operation STACK Python · Resolve scripting API · JSON configs STATUS In active use

Hours of daily dailies-timeline setup in Resolve, collapsed to one click: EDL once, then append-only mornings.

SLATE PROBLEM BUILD PROOF NEXT

Problem

Overnight renders land across hundreds of shots, and I was rebuilding dailies timelines before the first review. On a busy show that was hours, every morning. Conform the cut once, then only append what changed.

What I built

Takes an EDL onceThe cut comes in once and becomes the master review timeline. After that the tool maintains it.
Scan by departmentWalks the render folders and sorts new media by department: animation, lighting, compositing, matte painting, editorial temps.
Config as memoryEach department is a JSON config with a toggle. Newly discovered departments are added disabled.
Append, don't rebuildA registry remembers which timeline each department feeds. Every run appends only what's new.
Colour as languageEach clip gets a Resolve colour by department, so the timeline reads at a glance in review.

Three panels share that config and one EDL registry: Scan & Import, Quick Import, and Department Import. An EDL-aware render panel is on deck.

The morning batch: scan and append

Quick import: one render, placed on the right track

Most of the day is one re-rendered shot. Paste a path, confirm the parse, and the clip lands on the first clear track at the EDL record-in, stacked above the old version.

Department import: the whitelist, by department

Nothing is hardcoded per show. Each department is whitelisted per format, and a new one arrives switched off until you turn it on.

On deck: an EDL-aware render tab

The registry already has every shot's in and out. Pick a preset, queue the batch, and hand it to Resolve. On deck: designed and being built. The parsing and placement already ship.

What it shows

  • Department split, timeline shape, and colour tags follow how reviews actually run.
  • Show root, EDL, and render types are config, so the next show starts from one EDL.
  • Unknown media is flagged and never auto-imported.
