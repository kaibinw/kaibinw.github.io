Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

1. Who I am

I am Kai-Bin Wong, a sole trader in Brisbane, Australia, operating under ABN 35 840 410 536. This policy covers kaibinw.github.io. Contact: wongkaibin8080@gmail.com.

2. What I collect

  • Your email address, when you type it into the Baseline Core download form, the Starter Blueprint notify form or the Operator Kit waitlist form, and when you pay through Stripe Checkout. I also record which form you used.
  • Order details from Stripe for paid orders: your email, what you bought, the amount and the date. Stripe holds your payment details. I never see your card number.
  • Visit counts. The site uses GoatCounter, which counts page views and clicks such as a download or a signup. It sets no cookies and does not track you across sites.

Your browser also keeps a small note on your own device, for 24 hours, that you have just submitted a form, so the same address is not sent twice. It never leaves your device.

3. What I use it for

Only three things: delivering your order, sending your receipt, and telling you about major releases and new tiers. Nothing else.

4. Email and the Spam Act

I email you only because you asked, by submitting a form or buying. There are no drip sequences and no automated marketing funnels. I do not sell your details or share them with third parties for their own use.

Every email carries a way to unsubscribe, it works in one step, and I act on it immediately. You can also email me at any time and I will remove you.

5. Who handles your data

Email addresses from the forms are kept in a private Google Sheet, written through a Google Apps Script. Payments run on Stripe. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visit counts run on GoatCounter. These providers may process data outside Australia. Each has its own privacy policy.

6. How long I keep it

Email addresses stay until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them. Order records are kept for as long as Australian tax law requires, which is currently five years.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what I hold about you by emailing me. If you are unhappy with how I handled a request you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

8. Changes

If I change this policy I will update the date at the top. The Terms of Sale cover refunds and licensing.
